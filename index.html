Sources and Methodology

Rigorous definitions, research standards, and explicit boundaries governing this prototype.

Analytical Rigor Standard

1. Operational Definition of a GCC

For the purpose of GCC Atlas, a Global Capability Center (GCC) is defined strictly as a company-controlled India capability unit serving its global parent organization.

  • Parent Counting Standard: The global corporate parent is counted once. Discrete regional campuses, physical operating facilities, and legal entities in India are mapped separately under the parent.
  • Exclusion of Third-Party Outsourcers: Third-party IT/BPO service providers (e.g., TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture) are strictly excluded from the peer database.
  • Footprint Classification: Evidence distinguishes clearly between public intent announcements, confirmed operating facilities, and subsequent campus expansions.
  • Job Posting Rule: Job postings or recruiter listings alone do not establish a verified GCC footprint, as they frequently indicate pilot hiring, contractor staffing, or speculative mandates.

2. Evidence Hierarchy & Claims Verification

Every claim in this workspace is linked to inspectable source material. We maintain an explicit distinction between corporate self-reported statements and independent verification:

  • Company Source Supports Claim: Official corporate press releases, verified newsroom statements, and primary corporate portals. These confirm corporate presence and geographic locations.
  • Independently Verified: Corporate entity filings (Ministry of Corporate Affairs / MCA), audited regulatory disclosures, or registered property deeds.
  • Undated Sources: Websites without explicit publication timestamps remain labeled as Undated current website as of the research check date (12 October 2026). Timestamps are never fabricated.
  • Analyst Classification: Functional categorizations inferred from career listings or secondary profiles are explicitly marked as Analyst classification.

3. Prototype Scope & Implementation Boundaries

This prototype is engineered specifically for the Flexiple Product Intern assignment authored by Andluru Sai Punith Reddy:

  • Curated Data: Exactly four seed company records researched on 12 October 2026. Does not claim exhaustive coverage of India's 1,600+ GCCs.
  • Browser-Local State: Shortlist and scenario modifications are stored locally in the user's browser (localStorage) with defensive corruption recovery. No account, API keys, or server authentication required.
  • Deterministic Cost Scenario: Cost models use transparent, reproducible arithmetic formulas. No dynamic algorithmic black boxes or hidden variables.
  • AI Disclosure: Generative AI and agent tools were leveraged to accelerate code architecture and research formatting; however, no live LLM inference runs inside this prototype runtime. Automated AI extraction is documented on the future product roadmap.
